## Recognise the shape

Most estimation questions begin with demand, supply, capacity, or replacement cycles. Identify the pattern before selecting your starting point.

## Traps to avoid

- Registered units are not the same as active units.
- Average demand does not describe peak demand.
- People, households, trips, and transactions are different units.
- A percentage should come with a reason, not false precision.

## Prepare for a follow-up

After your first result, explain which assumption matters most. Be ready to change it, compare two approaches, or separate urban and rural behaviour.
