## What interviewers are really testing

A guesstimate is not a maths test. The interviewer wants to see whether you can break a vague problem into parts, make reasonable assumptions, and explain the calculation clearly.

## The five steps

1. **Clarify** the quantity, geography, and time period.
2. **Structure** the problem using a top-down or bottom-up approach.
3. **Segment** into three to five groups that do not overlap.
4. **Estimate** using round numbers and explain each assumption.
5. **Sanity-check** the result against a familiar reference.

> A round number with a clear reason is better than a precise-looking guess.

## Common mistakes

- Starting the calculation before agreeing on scope.
- Double-counting overlapping groups.
- Mixing daily, monthly, and annual quantities.
- Finishing without checking the answer.
