## Start with scope

Before you touch a number, ask what is being measured, where, and for which period. A clear scope prevents a neat calculation from answering the wrong question.

## Choose your structure

- **Top-down:** begin with a large population and apply sensible filters.
- **Bottom-up:** begin with one unit and scale it up.
- Split into a few groups that do not overlap and cover the whole problem.

> Say your structure out loud before calculating.

## Estimate and check

Use round numbers, give one reason for each assumption, and compare the final result with something you know. These are placeholder learning notes for the initial release.
